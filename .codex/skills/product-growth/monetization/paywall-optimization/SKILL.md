# Paywall optimization

Make the purchase decision understandable and valuable, then test it across the full subscription lifecycle.

## Workflow

1. Identify the user's job, value already experienced, entitlement being offered, and moment the paywall appears.
2. Audit benefit hierarchy, product scope, price, billing interval, renewal, trial, cancellation, restore-purchase, close, and accessibility behavior.
3. Separate hypotheses for timing, message, creative, offer, layout, and price so the mechanism remains interpretable.
4. Choose cohorts by legitimate context such as lifecycle stage or job, not sensitive inference.
5. Measure paywall reach, purchase or trial start, paid conversion, revenue per eligible user, refunds, chargebacks, churn, retention, and support contacts.
6. Run platform and storefront billing checks before implementing payment-route changes.
7. Ship only when practical value and downstream guardrails pass.

## Deliverable

Return:
- paywall clarity and friction audit
- hypothesis backlog by lever
- variant and measurement plan
- subscription-lifecycle scorecard
- compliance and rollback checklist

## Guardrails

- Never hide material terms or obstruct dismissal and cancellation.
- Do not call a high initial conversion a win without paid retention and refund data.
- Do not copy a winning creative or placement from another category as a rule.
