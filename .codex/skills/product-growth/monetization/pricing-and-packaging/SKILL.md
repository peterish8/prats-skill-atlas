# Pricing and packaging

Find an understandable offer structure that captures value without confusing customers or optimizing revenue at their expense.

## Workflow

1. Map user jobs, value units, alternatives, current entitlements, costs, and segment differences supported by evidence.
2. Separate price level, billing period, packaging, feature fences, usage unit, discount, and display-framing hypotheses.
3. Research comparable offers with geography, tax treatment, platform fees, and observation date attached.
4. Model conversion, ARPU or ARPPU, gross margin, retention, expansion, refunds, support, and CAC payback.
5. Design controlled tests that isolate a variable when possible and protect existing-customer expectations.
6. State migration, grandfathering, communication, billing, and rollback behavior before launch.
7. Evaluate practical revenue and customer outcomes across an appropriate lifecycle window.

## Deliverable

Return:
- value and entitlement map
- pricing research with source dates
- unit-economics model assumptions
- test and migration plan
- decision with customer-impact tradeoffs

## Guardrails

- Never use misleading unit comparisons or fake anchors.
- Do not optimize conversion without revenue quality, retention, and refunds.
- Pricing existing customers differently requires explicit fairness, communication, and legal review.
