# User interview analysis

Produce a synthesis that preserves what participants actually said while making patterns, counterexamples, and decision implications easy to inspect.

## Workflow

1. Define the research questions and decision before coding notes.
2. Normalize notes by participant and moment without deleting uncertainty or emotionally relevant context.
3. Code observed behavior, stated need, trigger, barrier, workaround, outcome, quote, and researcher inference separately.
4. Cluster evidence by user circumstance and journey stage; count participants only when the sample and method make counts meaningful.
5. Actively search for contradictions, negative cases, leading-question effects, and sampling gaps.
6. Create findings only when multiple evidence points support them; attach source IDs and confidence.
7. Convert findings into decisions, unresolved questions, and hypotheses for further research or experiments.

## Deliverable

Return:
- research-question coverage map
- coded evidence table
- themes with participant traceability
- contradictions and sampling limits
- decision implications and next research

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate or clean up participant quotes.
- Do not report qualitative frequency as population prevalence.
- Keep observations, participant interpretation, and analyst inference distinct.
