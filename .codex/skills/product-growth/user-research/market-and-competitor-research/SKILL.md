# Market and competitor research

Understand the real choice set, underserved user outcomes, acquisition language, business models, and evidence gaps around a growth decision.

## Workflow

1. Define the market by user job and circumstance before naming competitors.
2. Separate direct products, indirect substitutes, manual workarounds, nonconsumption, and status quo.
3. Search primary sources first: product pages, pricing, policies, filings, app-store listings, changelogs, and first-party documentation.
4. Inspect acquisition promise, onboarding path, activation moment, monetization, retention hooks, and cancellation where access is authorized.
5. Record observation date, geography, platform, plan, and source URL because market evidence changes.
6. Compare claims and interfaces without asserting performance unless verified data supports it.
7. Turn patterns into differentiated hypotheses and research questions, not a feature parity backlog.

## Deliverable

Return:
- market definition and alternatives map
- source-linked competitor matrix
- promise-to-value journey comparisons
- pricing and packaging observations
- opportunities, risks, and hypotheses

## Guardrails

- Never bypass access controls or create paid accounts without authority.
- Do not present scraped or estimated metrics as verified.
- Avoid copying protected content, branding, or manipulative patterns.
