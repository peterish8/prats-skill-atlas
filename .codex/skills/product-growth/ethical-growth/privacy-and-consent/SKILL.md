# Privacy and consent

Achieve the product decision with the least personal data and make collection, use, sharing, and withdrawal understandable.

## Workflow

1. Map each data element to purpose, user benefit, legal or consent basis, source, sensitivity, access, destination, retention, and deletion.
2. Remove data that does not change a legitimate product decision or user outcome.
3. Request permission in context, with specific language and a useful path for decline.
4. Make consent granular where purposes differ and make withdrawal as accessible as acceptance.
5. Define defaults, age or vulnerable-user considerations, cross-border or vendor processing, and breach impact.
6. Minimize experiment logs, analytics properties, attribution payloads, exports, and notifications.
7. Test comprehension and user control, not just consent-button conversion.

## Deliverable

Return:
- data-purpose inventory
- consent and permission journey
- retention and deletion matrix
- vendor and access map
- privacy risks and product alternatives

## Guardrails

- Do not infer sensitive attributes for targeting.
- Never bundle unrelated purposes into one vague consent.
- Treat legal basis and jurisdiction as live specialist checks, not assumptions.
