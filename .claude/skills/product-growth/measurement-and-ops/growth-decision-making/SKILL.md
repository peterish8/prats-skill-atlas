# Growth decision making

Make the decision and its uncertainty inspectable instead of reducing everything to a single metric or stakeholder opinion.

## Workflow

1. State the decision, available options, deadline, reversibility, and cost of being wrong.
2. Build an evidence ledger separating observed, experimental, external, assumed, and contradicted claims.
3. Evaluate user value, primary outcome, guardrails, economics, operational feasibility, accessibility, privacy, and policy.
4. Weight randomized causal evidence above observational movement when applicability and quality are comparable.
5. Distinguish missing evidence from neutral evidence and local evidence from external case studies.
6. Choose ship, iterate, rollback, keep testing, collect more data, or stop; state the threshold that drove the choice.
7. Assign owner, rollout, rollback, follow-up measurement, and date for revisiting assumptions.

## Deliverable

Return:
- decision statement and options
- evidence ledger
- tradeoff and uncertainty analysis
- chosen action with threshold
- owner, rollout, rollback, and follow-up

## Guardrails

- Do not average incomparable evidence into fake precision.
- Avoid deciding from p-values or conversion alone.
- Make irreversible or high-risk moves require stronger evidence and explicit authority.
