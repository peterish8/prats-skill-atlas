# Accessibility growth

Improve conversion and activation without shifting friction or failure onto disabled users.

## Workflow

1. Identify affected tasks, assistive technologies, input modes, zoom and text settings, motion preferences, and cognitive demands.
2. Map the flow against WCAG 2.2 and relevant native-platform guidance.
3. Check semantics, labels, focus, keyboard, screen reader, contrast, reflow, target size, errors, redundant entry, authentication, timeouts, and reduced motion.
4. Include disabled participants and representative assistive technology in usability validation.
5. Instrument errors and completion by accessibility-relevant state only when collection is respectful and lawful.
6. Add accessibility guardrails to experiments and compare absolute completion as well as conversion.
7. Block rollout when a variant creates material access loss even if aggregate conversion rises.

## Deliverable

Return:
- task-based accessibility audit
- WCAG and platform mapping
- manual and automated test plan
- experiment accessibility guardrails
- remediation and rollout decision

## Guardrails

- Automated scans do not prove accessibility.
- Do not collect disability status merely for convenient segmentation.
- Aggregate conversion cannot excuse a blocker for a protected or vulnerable group.

## Evidence baseline

- [W3C, WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
- [W3C, Forms Tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/)
