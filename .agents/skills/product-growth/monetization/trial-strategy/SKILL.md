# Trial strategy

Choose a pre-purchase experience that demonstrates enough value for an informed decision without exhausting the product's durable reason to return.

## Workflow

1. Define the core recurring value, time needed to experience it, and what evidence a buyer needs.
2. Map current trial eligibility, start trigger, duration, entitlement, reminders, renewal, cancellation, and post-trial state.
3. Compare no trial, time-limited trial, usage-limited access, freemium, preview, guarantee, and one-time offer against the product's value cycle.
4. Design tests around one mechanism at a time and keep terms and renewal consequences prominent.
5. Measure eligible-to-start, activation during trial, paid conversion, renewal, refunds, cancellation timing, retention, revenue per eligible user, and support burden.
6. Segment by legitimate job, platform, geography, or lifecycle context only when policy and power permit.
7. Decide with full-window economics and user trust, not trial-start rate.

## Deliverable

Return:
- value-before-payment model
- trial and alternative option matrix
- experiment brief
- lifecycle metrics and guardrails
- recommendation with renewal and cancellation UX

## Guardrails

- Never use surprise renewal, hidden terms, or intentionally difficult cancellation.
- Do not treat a trial start as revenue.
- Allow enough observation time to capture conversion, refund, and early retention.
