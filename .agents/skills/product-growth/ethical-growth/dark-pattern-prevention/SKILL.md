# Dark-pattern prevention

Replace coercive conversion tactics with transparent patterns that preserve informed, reversible user choice.

## Workflow

1. Map the decisions users make and the information, controls, timing, and consequences visible at each point.
2. Check for disguised ads, fake scarcity, confirmshaming, preselection, hidden fees, misdirection, forced continuity, roach motels, sneaking, and privacy steering.
3. Compare visual weight, clicks, reading burden, defaults, and reversibility across accept and decline paths.
4. Verify total price, renewal, trial conversion, cancellation, data use, and material limitations before commitment.
5. Review experiment incentives so the team cannot declare manipulative lift a clean win.
6. Design a transparent alternative and test comprehension, regret, complaints, cancellation, and trust.
7. Escalate consumer-protection uncertainty and preserve evidence of the review.

## Deliverable

Return:
- choice-architecture audit
- identified pattern and user harm
- transparent replacement design
- trust and complaint guardrails
- ship, revise, or block recommendation

## Guardrails

- Never recommend deceptive tactics as optimization ideas.
- Do not hide behind legal text when the main interface is misleading.
- A short-term conversion increase does not outweigh uninformed or trapped consent.
