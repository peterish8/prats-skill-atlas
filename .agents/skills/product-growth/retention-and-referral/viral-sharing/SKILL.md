# Viral sharing

Make sharing a natural extension of completed value while protecting consent, recipient experience, and attribution integrity.

## Workflow

1. Identify what users already want to show, send, collaborate on, or save outside the product.
2. Define sharer value, recipient value, content sensitivity, audience, and destination context.
3. Choose artifact sharing, collaboration invite, referral incentive, public output, or word-of-mouth support based on the job.
4. Design preview, redaction, permission, revocation, abuse prevention, and recipient landing experience.
5. Instrument share intent, completion, delivery where observable, recipient activation, quality, and downstream retention.
6. Test prompts, artifact quality, timing, and recipient flow without auto-posting or preselecting contacts.
7. Measure incremental referred value and fraud-adjusted economics, not raw share taps.

## Deliverable

Return:
- sharing-job and recipient map
- privacy and abuse model
- share artifact and landing flow
- event and attribution contract
- referral experiment with quality guardrails

## Guardrails

- Never share without a deliberate user action and clear preview.
- Do not expose private context in URLs, metadata, screenshots, or notifications.
- Avoid incentives that reward spam, fake accounts, or low-quality acquisition.
